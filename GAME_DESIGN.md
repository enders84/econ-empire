# 🏛️ Econ Empire
## Game Design Document (Version 1.0)

---

# Vision

Econ Empire is an educational grand strategy game where the player governs a modern nation through economic policymaking.

Players manage fiscal policy, monetary policy, government spending, taxation, trade, and political approval while responding to economic crises, technological innovation, and international events.

The objective is to create a fun strategy game that naturally teaches macroeconomics through decision-making.

---

# Core Gameplay Loop

Each game quarter follows this cycle:

1. Review economic indicators
2. Read current events and news
3. Adjust government policies
4. End the quarter
5. Run the economic simulation
6. View updated results
7. Repeat

# Educational Goals

Players should develop an understanding of:

- Aggregate Demand
- Aggregate Supply
- Fiscal Policy
- Monetary Policy
- Inflation
- Unemployment
- Business Cycles
- Public Finance
- International Trade

The game should explain *why* economic outcomes occur, helping players connect their policy choices to economic theory.
